Keep Your Mac Awake for Morning Briefings

UHI prepares your daily morning briefing at your scheduled time. Follow these simple steps so your Mac stays awake:

1
Open Apple Menu → System Settings

Click the Apple menu () in the top-left corner of your Mac and select System Settings.

2
Click Lock Screen or Energy Saver

In the left sidebar, click Lock Screen (or Energy Saver on desktops).

3
Prevent Automatic Sleeping

Turn on "Prevent automatic sleeping on power adapter when the display is off", or choose a longer sleep timer.

💡 Grandma Tip: Keep your Mac plugged in overnight so your morning briefing is fresh and waiting for you!


Turn On Auto-Start on Your Mac

Launch UHI automatically whenever your Mac powers on or logs in:

1
Open System Settings → General

Click the Apple menu () → System Settings, then click General in the sidebar.

2
Click Login Items

Select Login Items from the General menu list.

3
Add UHI to "Open at Login"

Under "Open at Login", click the + button, select UHI from Applications, and click Open.

⚡ Instant Setup: You can also mark it complete right here once configured!
